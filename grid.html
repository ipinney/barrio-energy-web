Try it

Connect

MCP server (streamable HTTP, OAuth sign-in with a free account):

https://grid.barrioenergy.com/mcp
  • Claude: Settings, Connectors, Add custom connector, paste the URL, leave the OAuth client fields blank, press Connect and sign in with your email and a 6 digit code.
  • ChatGPT: Settings, Apps and Connectors, Create, paste the URL, authentication OAuth, sign in when prompted.
  • Muse or any other agent: give it the URL above or this page and it can build the skill itself. A ready-made skill file is at /grid/skill.md.

REST, same tools, GET with query parameters or POST JSON, with the key from your account:

curl -H "Authorization: Bearer $GRID_KEY" "https://grid.barrioenergy.com/v1/nearest_substation?address=Edna,%20TX&min_kv=138" curl -H "Authorization: Bearer $GRID_KEY" "https://grid.barrioenergy.com/v1/find_powered_land?county=Jackson&min_acres=50&max_sub_mi=1" curl "https://grid.barrioenergy.com/v1/tools"

Tools

ToolWhat it answers
grid_contextEverything around one site: county, likely serving utility, 3 nearest substations, transmission lines, ERCOT queue projects and planned transmission upgrades within 25 miles, and railroads: the nearest rail lines and whether one lies between the site and its substation.
nearest_substationClosest substations to an address or coordinate, with voltage, owner and distance in miles. Filter by minimum kV. Also returns planned, unbuilt substations nearby (built:false, status, docket, expected date, pin confidence and the docket's location caveat) and warns when a planned and a built station share a name.
find_powered_landParcels near a qualifying substation, by county or point and radius, with acreage, owner and owner type (company, individual, trust or estate, public), situs address, legal description, appraised values and distance to the substation. Set group_by_owner to rank landowners by total acres near the substation. Enterprise; Free and Pro get parcel and owner counts.
parcel_ownerWho owns land at an address, around a named substation or by property ID: property ID, acres, situs address, legal description and appraisal values. Owner name and mailing address on Enterprise. Each parcel carries owner_class (university_lands, state, federal, county, city, school_district, special_district, railroad, utility, company, individual, trust_estate), undivided_interest when several owners share one property ID, city_limits_per_cad from the ICL/OCL legal token, and value_status (not_published_by_county means zeros are missing data, not a $0 parcel).
jurisdictionWho governs a site: county, inside city limits (which city), estimated ETJ, nearest city, school district, groundwater district or rule of capture, load zone, likely utility, and whether the site is in a Qualified Opportunity Zone now and from 2027. cad_city_limits compares the appraisal roll's ICL/OCL legal token for the parcel at the point with the boundary layer and warns when they disagree.
pipelines_nearGas pipelines near a site from Railroad Commission permits: operator, system, nominal diameter, interstate or intrastate, distance, miles of line in the radius. Nearest 12-inch-plus and nearest interstate line, plus trunklines, compressor stations, processing plant capacity and EIA pipeline capacity projects within 15 miles.
fiber_nearFiber near a site: routes by provider with distance (provider GIS paths and carrier long-haul corridors), the nearest carrier hotels, colo and data centers, and fiber-to-premises providers from the FCC. Pro adds fiber counts, aerial or underground placement, route and facility names and speeds.
industrial_neighborsHeavy industry already operating nearby (TCEQ emissions inventory) and recent TCEQ air permit applications, including data centers and gas plants before they reach the ERCOT queue.
site_screenEnterprise. One-call screen for a data center, battery, generator or industrial load: grid, prices, queue crowding, gas, jurisdiction, neighbors and the parcel at the point with its owner and that owner's nearby holdings, with a flags list (including railroads between the site and its substation) and a 0-100 score using stated weights. Free and Pro get the score, flag counts, county, load zone and nearest substation.
search_gridFind a substation, company, planned project, battery (by developer, POI or INR), air permit, queue project, pricing node, city or county by name, with coordinates.
substation_detailEverything about one substation: voltage, owner, lines, batteries interconnecting there, generation_queue_at_poi (total projects, mw_total, projects by fuel/mw/study_phase), queue, prices, an inferred station class (distribution, transmission, switching, generation tie, customer-owned) with its evidence, and how likely_utility was derived with a flag when it disagrees with the station owner. Planned upgrades on Pro; surrounding landowners on Enterprise.
grid_projectsPro. Planned new substations, approved lines and upgrades near a site, in a county or by utility, with status, expected date, cost, docket and sources.
air_permitsPro. TCEQ generation and data-center air permits statewide, by county or near a site, pending first.
whats_newPro. Battery milestones, queue entries, permits and new grid projects near a site or in a county over the last N days.
ercot_queueERCOT GIS report generation and storage projects near a site, in a county, or at a POI substation: INR, project name, fuel, tech, MW, county, zone, POI substation, study phase, COD, entity. Summary with mw_by_fuel and mw_by_stage. Filter by fuel (SOL, WIN, GAS, etc.). Free: fuel, county, zone, distance_mi only. Pro: full project details.
battery_queueBattery storage projects in the ERCOT GIS report by site, county or point of interconnection: developer, MW, POI substation, study stage, signed IA, projected COD.
power_plantsOperating and planned power plants and batteries from EIA-860M near a site or in a county: operator, technology, MW, storage MWh and duration, operating year, planned retirements.
large_loadsLarge loads planned in a county or around a site: announced data center campuses with coordinates and a 75 MW large load flag, campuses within 25 miles across county lines, load-driven transmission projects from ERCOT TPIT, data center air permits, local news and Grid Report mentions, with load zone and utility. Ranks all 254 counties by signal. ERCOT does not publish its large load queue by county; this is the public evidence.
data_centersTexas data center projects near a site or in a county: every location free. Names, companies, status, MW, acres, capex, utility and substation, abatements, opposition and sources on Pro. 364 projects on 274 campuses across 101 counties, compiled from utility and PUCT filings, county and city records, abatement agreements, TDLR and TCEQ records and news.
company_footprintWhich companies are building data centers across Texas, ranked by project count with total MW, counties, and how many are operating or under construction. Or every project for one company. Pro.
local_sentimentHow a Texas county or city treats data centers: a score from official actions since September 2024 (abatements, zoning votes, development agreements, moratoria, zoning denials, withdrawals after opposition), each project counted once, 12-month half-life, plus recent actions with votes and sources. Community opposition and lawsuits are listed, not scored. Counties and cities scored separately. Pro.
data_center_newsLocal and trade news on Texas data center projects near a site or in a county: headline, outlet, date and link, placed at the project, town or county and tagged by topic. Summaries on Pro.
node_pricesERCOT day-ahead prices in $/MWh, hourly, for the load zone at a site, the nearest resource nodes and the trading hubs, with 7-day averages, peak and off-peak, spikes and negative hours. Or one settlement point's full series.
volatile_nodesNodes ranked for storage and solar: TB2 and TB4 spreads, annualized arbitrage per MW, volatility, evening peak, solar capture price, node-versus-zone basis, real-time TB4, DART, and queued storage within 10 miles of each node. Statewide, by load zone, or within a radius of a site.
ancillary_pricesERCOT ancillary service clearing prices (RegUp, RegDown, RRS, ECRS, Non-Spin) with a battery AS stack and monthly history back to 2024.
grid_newsLatest articles from The Grid Report, searchable: ERCOT, Batch Zero and large loads, PUCT dockets, transmission, data center and battery development. Full text by article id. Free and unmetered.
my_projectsYour own sites in three lists: Properties, Prospects and Saved places, imported from KMZ, KML or GeoJSON or saved on the map. Lists them or reads one project; Pro screens up to 25 sites and ranks them. Private to your account.
save_placeSaves a point to your Saved places with a name and note when you ask your assistant to pin a location. Returns a map link.
upgrade_to_proPro checkout link. Muse and other agents can buy Pro for a user with Link after the user approves.

Inputs take a street address, a Texas city or county name, or lat + lng. Distances are in miles. Texas only. Answers with a location include map_url, a link to that spot on the map.

Data

  • Substations and transmission: HIFLD, with owners enriched by Barrio.
  • Generation and storage queue: ERCOT GIS reports.
  • Planned transmission and substations: Barrio research from ERCOT RPG and PUCT filings.
  • Prices: ERCOT public API day-ahead and real-time settlement point prices and ancillary service clearing prices, refreshed hourly.
  • Battery queue: ERCOT GIS report, monthly.
  • Jurisdiction: TxDOT and StratMap city limits, Census school districts, TWDB groundwater districts. ETJ is an estimate from the statutory distance.
  • Opportunity Zones: Treasury CDFI Fund QOZ 1.0 tracts (expire 31 Dec 2026) and the 605 QOZ 2.0 tracts the Governor nominated on 4 Sep 2026 for 2027 to 2036, shown as nominated until Treasury certifies them.
  • Pipelines: Railroad Commission of Texas T-4 permits. Compressor stations: HIFLD/ORNL and TCEQ emissions inventory. Processing plants: HIFLD/EIA-757. Pipeline capacity projects: EIA. Industrial neighbors: TCEQ emissions inventory and air permits.
  • Parcels: TNRIS StratMap 2025 with appraisal district owner and value fields. Statewide load in progress; each answer lists the counties covered.
