Snapboard Privacy Policy

Effective October 19, 2026. Snapboard is published by Barrio Resources LLC, 1129 E 6th 1/2 St, Houston, TX 77009 ("we").

Typing

Everything you type with Snapboard is processed on your phone. Word suggestions and learned words stay on your phone. Your typing is never sent to us or anyone else.

AI images (subscription)

The image key is a paid subscription sold through Google Play. When you tap Generate, the description you typed in the image box is sent to our server and then to our image provider (OpenRouter, which routes to Google's image models) to create the image. We receive:

  • The image description you typed, and the generated image's size and model. Descriptions are kept for up to 30 days to prevent abuse and handle reports, then deleted.
  • Your Google Play purchase token, used only to confirm your subscription with Google and count images used in your billing period.
  • Reports you submit about an image.

We do not receive your name, email, contacts, location or anything you type outside the image box. We do not sell data or use it for advertising. Generated images are not stored on our server; they are sent to your phone and deleted from it automatically after 24 hours unless you insert or save them.

OpenRouter: openrouter.ai/privacy. Google: policies.google.com/privacy.

Payments

Subscriptions are billed and managed by Google Play. We never see your payment details. Cancel anytime in Google Play > Payments & subscriptions.

Permissions

Internet: used only by the image key. Contacts (optional): used on the phone to suggest names while typing, never uploaded. Vibrate: key feedback.

Content rules

Don't use the image key to create sexual content involving minors, sexual content of real people, content that harasses or threatens others, or illegal content. Images can be reported from the image screen; we review reports and may end access for abuse.

Children

Snapboard is not directed to children under 13.

Deleting your Snapboard data

Snapboard (by Barrio Resources LLC) has no user accounts. Uninstalling Snapboard removes all of its data from your phone.

To delete the records on our server tied to your subscription (image descriptions, image counts and reports):

  1. Email ivan@barrioenergy.com with the subject "Snapboard data deletion".
  2. Include the Google Play order number of your subscription (it starts with GPA. and is in your Google Play purchase receipt email).
  3. We delete those records within 30 days and reply to confirm. Image descriptions are removed automatically after 30 days in any case.
