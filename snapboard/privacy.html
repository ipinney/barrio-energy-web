Snapboard Privacy Policy

Effective October 19, 2026. Snapboard is published by Barrio Resources LLC, 1129 E 6th 1/2 St, Houston, TX 77009 ("we").

What Snapboard collects

Nothing. Snapboard has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no servers of ours. We do not receive, store or sell anything you type.

Typing

Everything you type is processed on your phone. Word suggestions and learned words stay on your phone. Typing is never sent over the network.

AI image generation (optional)

The image key works only if you add your own API key from Google, OpenAI or xAI. Your keys are stored on your phone only. When you tap Generate, the description you typed in the image box is sent directly from your phone to the provider you selected, using your key. That provider processes it under its own terms and privacy policy and bills your account with them. We never see your prompts, images or keys.

  • Google: policies.google.com/privacy
  • OpenAI: openai.com/policies/privacy-policy
  • xAI: x.ai/legal/privacy-policy

Generated images are saved temporarily on your phone so they can be inserted, and are deleted automatically after 24 hours.

Permissions

Internet: used only by the image key, to reach the provider you chose. Contacts (optional): used on the phone to suggest names while typing, never uploaded. Vibrate: key feedback.

Children

Snapboard is not directed to children under 13.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling Snapboard removes all of its data, including your API keys, from your phone.
