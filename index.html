Small sites, real power, local footprint

Barrio Energy builds powered industrial sites of one to fifty acres in Texas towns with spare grid capacity. Our tenants run containerized computing and battery storage that can start, stop and shed load when the grid needs it, which is why ERCOT counts them as flexible demand rather than a strain on it.

We pay property taxes in the counties we build in, we lease from local landowners, and we publish the grid data we work from so anyone can check it. Questions from neighbors and officials go to info@barrioenergy.com.
