Team

One principal in Houston, a set of purpose-built software agents that keep the books, the contracts and the research straight, and a human decision behind every commitment.

Ivan Pinney

Principal, Barrio Energy

Ivan started Barrio Energy in Houston after a career at Chevron. Barrio buys industrial land near the Texas grid, brings power to it, and leases it to digital infrastructure, battery storage and industrial tenants. The first powered land project began in 2021; the portfolio now spans eight sites from West Texas to the Gulf Coast.

He works directly with landowners, utilities, county officials and tenants on every site, and questions from neighbors, officials and reporters reach him through info@barrioenergy.com. He also runs FrontierGen, which develops larger energy and digital infrastructure projects.

Ivan lives in Houston with his wife and three children and coaches youth soccer.

How we work

Barrio is deliberately small. Instead of a back office, we run purpose-built software agents for accounting, legal review, tax, operations and market research. Each one proposes; a person decides. Nothing is filed, signed, sent or posted to the books without Ivan seeing it first. The agents below are the ones that do that work.
